How many do you need?

One home

One label per bin. Most houses need one or two - the trash bin, plus recycling if you want that marked too.

A few bins

Multi-packs cover the whole set and cost less per label. Current pack pricing is at primocans.com.

A whole building

Landlords and managers: bulk pricing for labeling every bin on the property. Write info@primocans.com.

Putting it on

  1. Wipe the panel with rubbing alcohol and let it dry.
  2. Peel the backing off.
  3. Position on the bin's address space.
  4. Press hard from the center outward, about 30 seconds.
